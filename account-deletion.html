Nondh — Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your Nondh account and the data associated with it. Nondh is a site-management app for contractors to record workers, attendance, payments, materials, and related site data.

Option 1 — Delete your account from inside the app

The fastest way is directly in the app:

  1. Open Nondh and sign in.
  2. Tap the profile icon at the top right of the Overview screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and your data are permanently removed.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address linked to your account and we'll delete it for you:

Email: support@nondh.com
Subject: Account deletion request

Please send the request from the email address (or include the phone number) registered with your account, so we can verify it's you. We will action verified requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the data tied to it, including:

  • Your account details (email address and/or phone number).
  • All the business records you entered — workers, attendance, payments and ledgers, materials, suppliers, sites, contractors, and expenses.

What may be retained

We do not keep your business records after deletion. We may retain limited information only where required by law, or in anonymised/aggregated form that cannot identify you. Routine encrypted backups are cycled out within 90 days.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you only want to remove individual records rather than your whole account, you can delete them within the app without deleting your account.

Contact

Questions about deleting your data? Email support@nondh.com.
