Nondh — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Nondh ("we", "our", "the app") is a site-management tool for contractors to record workers, attendance, payments, materials, and related site data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using Nondh, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the app:

  • Account information: the email address and password you use to create an account. Passwords are handled securely by Google Firebase Authentication and are never stored by us in readable form.
  • Data you enter: the business records you add yourself — such as worker names and designations, attendance, payment and ledger amounts, materials, suppliers, sites, contractors, and expenses.

We do not collect your device location, contacts, camera, or photos, and we do not track your activity outside the app.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To save your records so they are available when you return, across your devices.
  • To provide the app's features (attendance, ledgers, reports, and so on).

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase and Cloud Firestore, part of Google Cloud. Google acts as our data processor and stores the data on secure servers. Each user's data is kept private to their own account — other users cannot see your records.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • With Google Firebase, solely to store and secure your data as described above.
  • If required by law, such as a valid legal request from authorities.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete records within the app at any time. If you want your entire account and all associated data deleted, contact us at the email below and we will remove it.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures provided by Google Firebase, including secure authentication and access rules that restrict each account's data to its owner. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children's privacy

Nondh is intended for use by contractors and businesses and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at:

Email: support@nondh.com

This policy applies to the Nondh mobile application. Your continued use of the app means you accept this policy.
